# Piracy Guide

## Before you Pirate
There is a deep and vast world of intentionally free stuff on the internet. Through my experience I've found that its just better to search first for a free alternative for the paid thing you desire rather than going through a whole process of tinkering. But if you need to continue then for sure make sure you [prepare](#internet-saftey) yourself.

## Internet Saftey
You don't need to be pirating for these tips.

### Install [uBlock Origin](https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock)
You can interact with the internet with just a browser but without additions you'll get into places you don't want to be in. Install [uBlock Origin](https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock). It is one of the best ad-blockers, but it'll also block entire unsafe sites and other bad stuff on the internet.

### Maybe Setup a VPN
If you are planning on torrenting then having a VPN and binding it to your torrent client is a must. Your ISP can track torrents and you can possibly get in trouble from them, which is usally just a letter or something asking you to not torrent. At worst the trouble can be just a blocking of internet or even rarer a fine.

If you do not plan to torrent then you really don't *need* a VPN.

### [Torrenting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BitTorrent)
Torrenting is the use of a torrent client to download files via a Peer 2 Peer connection with other users. With any torrent there will be Seeders and Leechers. Seeders are the users that are sharing the content. Leechers are the users that are recieving the content.

Torrenting itself is not a bad thing. But it's often associated with Piracy as its helpful in this area in being able to share larger files, so its not really used for anything other than that.
