# About
An about page for this wiki-like site.

## Why was this made?
I began this site after learning [FMHY](https://fmhy.net) uses [VitePress](https://vitepress.dev/) and was inspiried to use the same resource to make my own site. I've been looking for a way to start my own "wiki". I was not looking for anything like [VitePress](https://vitepress.dev/), but I instantly enjoyed it's ability to make sites with a cool style based mainly on markdown files.

## Is there a purpose for this site?
I do have one purpose in mind. To increase the spread of information. We live in a day and age where you can get *almost* any answer you could ask, and yet people spread a bunch of misinformation online. I wish to lead to information. Whether thats knowledge or a piece of software depends on the interacted source.

But I also just wanted a easy to access place to host my software, and to make that place my own rather than a big "boring-looking" github repo.
